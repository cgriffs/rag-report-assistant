# Harborline Energy Cooperative: Emissions Summary, Fiscal 2026

Note: Harborline Energy Cooperative is a fictional organization. This summary is sample data written for demonstration purposes.

## Overview

Harborline Energy Cooperative reported total Scope 1 and Scope 2 emissions of 48,210 tonnes of CO2 equivalent in fiscal 2026. That is a reduction of 6.4 percent compared with fiscal 2025, when emissions were 51,505 tonnes. The cooperative remains on track for its target of a 30 percent reduction by 2030 against the 2022 baseline.

## Scope 1 emissions

Scope 1 emissions were 31,870 tonnes, driven mainly by natural gas combustion at the Port Alice generation station. A burner upgrade completed in March 2026 reduced gas consumption at that station by 4 percent. Fleet emissions rose slightly because of an expanded service territory, adding about 210 tonnes.

## Scope 2 emissions

Scope 2 emissions were 16,340 tonnes using the market based method. The cooperative signed a renewable power purchase agreement in January 2026 that covers 40 percent of its purchased electricity. Under the location based method, Scope 2 emissions would have been 19,905 tonnes.

## Methane and other gases

Methane emissions from distribution infrastructure were estimated at 1,940 tonnes of CO2 equivalent. The leak detection and repair program surveyed 620 kilometres of pipeline in 2026 and repaired 87 leaks, double the number repaired in 2025.

## Next steps

Planned measures for fiscal 2027 include electrifying 25 percent of the light vehicle fleet and a second burner upgrade at the Mill Bay station. The cooperative also plans to publish Scope 3 estimates for purchased goods for the first time.
