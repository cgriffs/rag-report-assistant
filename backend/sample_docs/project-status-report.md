# Project Beacon: Status Report, October 2026

Note: Project Beacon is a fictional project at a fictional company. This report is sample data written for demonstration purposes.

## Summary

Project Beacon, the migration of the Atlas reporting platform to a cloud data warehouse, is 72 percent complete and tracking two weeks behind the original plan. The steering committee approved a revised go live date of February 16, 2027 at the October 9 meeting. The budget remains within tolerance, with 1.9 million dollars spent of the 2.6 million dollar approved budget.

## Completed milestones

Data pipeline migration for the finance and operations domains finished on September 30. User acceptance testing for the finance dashboards began on October 5 with 24 business users. The access control model, based on role based permissions, passed its security review on October 12 with no critical findings.

## Risks and issues

The main risk is the delayed delivery of historical emissions data from the legacy vendor, which is now expected on November 20. If that date slips again, the compliance reporting workstream will miss its December checkpoint. A second risk is tester availability during the December holiday period; the project team is recruiting two additional testers.

## Decisions needed

The steering committee is asked to approve 85,000 dollars for additional data quality tooling. The project manager recommends approval, because manual reconciliation is currently consuming about 30 hours per week of analyst time.

## Next milestones

Operations domain dashboards enter user acceptance testing on November 3. The training plan for 340 end users will be presented to the steering committee on November 18.
